# Known Issues

## All Devices

- Desktop Mode asks once per session before the right stick can move the
  mouse pointer. Select **Allow** and it works until the next restart.

## Snapdragon 8 Elite

- No audio over HDMI or DisplayPort yet.

## AYANEO Pocket S 1K / S 2K

- The AYANEO button isn't mapped yet.
- Touch is off on some units.

!!! note

    Something not listed here? [Open an issue](https://github.com/hashtagbasit/SteamOS-ARM-Port/issues)
    with your device and the [debug logs](troubleshooting.md#debug-logs).
